## About me

I’m a **vibe coder and prompt crafter**. I make vibe coding apps and websites by taking an idea, shaping the prompt, building the interface, wiring the product, and shipping the result.

I like experimenting with anime, AI, media, realtime features, playful interfaces, and anything that turns a rough thought into something people can actually use.

## What I build

- Vibe-coded web apps and product experiments
- Anime and media experiences
- AI-assisted tools and prompt-driven workflows
- Realtime interfaces and API-connected products
